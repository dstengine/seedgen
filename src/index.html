        How the seed phrase is made

        Everything runs in this page. The phrase comes from your device's own random generator, the addresses are derived on the spot, and nothing is sent anywhere — the page works with the network switched off, and once opened it opens again offline.

        The code is open: github.com/dstengine/seedgen. The derivation lives in src/wallet.ts, and the tests hold every chain to published reference addresses.

        A 24-word phrase is a standard BIP39 phrase that is also a valid TON mnemonic, so the same words open all five wallets. A TON mnemonic is always 24 words, so a 12-word phrase is BIP39 only: its TON address is the one Trust Wallet derives, and Tonkeeper will not take it.

        ChainPathOpens in

        A phrase is the wallet. Anyone who sees it can spend from it, and anything kept in a browser can be lost with it. For money that matters, write the words down on paper and keep them offline.
