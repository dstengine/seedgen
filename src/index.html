        How the seed phrase is made

        Everything runs in this page. The phrase comes from your device's own random generator, the addresses are derived on the spot, and nothing is sent anywhere — the page works with the network switched off.

        The 24 words are a standard BIP39 phrase that is also a valid TON mnemonic, so the same phrase opens all five wallets:

        A phrase is the wallet. Anyone who sees it can spend from it, and anything kept in a browser can be lost with it. For money that matters, write the words down on paper and keep them offline.
